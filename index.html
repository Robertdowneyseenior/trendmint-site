FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you only work with businesses in Lucknow?

No. We're based in Lucknow and work with businesses across India — strategy calls, reporting and creative reviews all happen online or on WhatsApp.

Can I hire you just for ads or just for video?

Yes. Each layer works on its own. We'll simply tell you where we see leaks in the rest of your funnel.

What is the WhatsApp Business API and why does it matter?

It's Meta's official way for businesses to message customers at scale from a verified number — with auto-replies, a shared team inbox and broadcasts. It's what lets every lead get a reply in seconds instead of hours. We run it through ChatWaba, our own platform.

How soon will I see leads?

Campaigns usually start bringing enquiries within the first one to two weeks of launch. The first month is about learning what converts; from there we scale what works.

What does the free strategy call include?

A 30-minute look at your current ads, content and follow-up, and a short plan of what we'd fix first. No obligation.
